Questions about DebtClear

About the app, your data and payoff estimates.

Is DebtClear free?

Yes, DebtClear is free to download and use on Android. The app includes advertising. You don’t need to create an account.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You enter your debts and record payments yourself. DebtClear doesn’t connect to your bank or move money. Keep your balances in line with your lender’s statements so the estimates stay useful.

What if I don’t know my interest rate?

You can add a debt and start tracking payments without knowing the interest rate. Add the rate when you have it. You’ll need it to calculate a payoff estimate. An unknown rate isn’t treated as zero interest.

How is my payoff date calculated?

The estimate uses the balances, interest rates, monthly budget, extra payments, and payment order you enter. Compare Snowball and Avalanche in the app. Estimates can change and don’t include every lender-specific fee or early repayment condition.

Debt Snowball vs Avalanche: what’s the difference?

Snowball directs extra payments towards the smallest balance first. Avalanche prioritizes the highest interest rate. DebtClear lets you compare estimated finish dates and interest using the same balances and monthly budget, then choose the payment order that fits your priorities.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Core debt tracking and payoff calculations work on your device without internet. Ads, analytics, and online services may use an internet connection. Debt details and payment records are stored locally on your device.
