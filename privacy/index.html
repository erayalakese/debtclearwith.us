Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026  ·  App: DebtClear (published as KrediErit on Google Play)  ·  Developer: Rooftop Online

Core debt tracking and payoff calculations work offline. Creditor names, balances, interest rates, and payment amounts are stored on your device and are not uploaded by the app to our servers or the Google services listed below. Push announcements, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising may use an internet connection and process technical identifiers. Device backups are described below.
1

About This App

DebtClear / KrediErit is a personal finance calculator application. It is not a bank, lender, credit counselor, debt settlement service, or financial institution of any kind. The app does not offer financial products, loan brokerage, or debt relief services.

DebtClear helps you track debts and payments, manage a monthly repayment budget, and estimate a payoff timeline using methods such as Debt Snowball or Debt Avalanche, based on numbers you enter yourself. All payoff calculations happen locally on your device.

2

Data You Enter — Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored locally in a private database on your device (SQLite via the Android data sandbox). The app does not upload this financial content to our servers or the third-party services described below:

  • Creditor names
  • Debt balances, interest rates, and minimum payment amounts
  • Your monthly payment budget
  • Payment history and payoff status you mark within the app
  • Your chosen payoff strategy

This data is not backed up to any cloud service by us. Android's standard auto-backup behavior may include it in your Google account backup depending on your device settings — this is controlled entirely by Android, not by us.

3

App Settings & Preferences

App settings — such as your selected display language, theme, payment reminder preference, and whether you have completed the onboarding tour — are stored locally using Android's SharedPreferences. We also store whether the notification permission prompt has already been shown. Firebase's installation and messaging identifiers are separate from these local preferences.

4

Payment Reminders, Push & In-App Notifications

Local payment reminders: If you enable payment reminders, the app schedules them directly on your device. The app does not send your reminder schedule or the financial details used to create it to Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn these reminders off using Payment reminders in the app's settings.

Push announcements: We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send app updates and announcements over the internet. On Android, an announcement can appear while the app is in the background or closed. If it arrives while the app is open, it is displayed through the device's notification system as a foreground notification. These messages use FCM even when displayed while you are using the app.

Your notification choices: On Android versions that require it, we request notification permission. You can change that permission or disable the App updates notification category in Android's settings for DebtClear / KrediErit. The app's Payment reminders switch controls local reminders, not push announcements. Denying or disabling notification display does not, by itself, stop Firebase from registering the app installation or delete messaging identifiers.

Notification text may be visible on your lock screen, depending on your device settings. You can hide notification content or disable lock-screen notifications in Android's settings.

5

Third-Party Services

DebtClear uses the Google services described below, which may process technical identifiers and usage data. The app does not attach your stored creditor names, balances, interest rates, or payment amounts to analytics events or push messaging requests.

Analytics
Firebase Analytics (Google)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate — for example, which screens are visited most, how long sessions last, and overall app stability. Firebase may collect:

  • App open / session duration events
  • Screen views and in-app navigation events
  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Country (derived from IP address, not stored as precise location)
  • A random Firebase installation ID (reset when you reinstall the app)

We record interaction events, such as adding a debt or recording a payment; batch actions may include an item count. We do not attach creditor names, balances, interest rates, payment amounts, or payment-history contents to these events.

Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Push Messaging
Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)

FCM uses a Firebase installation identifier and a messaging registration token to route app announcements to an installation. Google may also process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to operate the service. Notification titles and message bodies pass through Google's messaging infrastructure. Delivery and notification-open events may be reported through Firebase and Analytics.

The app may register with FCM when it starts, including when notification display permission has not been granted. Your debt records and local payment reminder schedule are not uploaded to FCM. Messaging identifiers and service data are handled under Firebase's retention and deletion policies; turning off notification display does not automatically delete them.

Learn more: Firebase privacy and data processing and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Crash Reporting
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

Crashlytics automatically sends a crash report to us if the app crashes. This helps us identify and fix bugs. A crash report may include:

  • Stack trace of the crash (code location only)
  • Device model, OS version, and app version at time of crash
  • Approximate time and frequency of the crash

Crash reports do not contain your name, debt data, or any personally identifiable information.

Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)

DebtClear displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads, Google may collect:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) — a resettable identifier on your device
  • Device identifiers and IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • General location inferred from IP address

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Privacy → Ads. If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6

Children's Privacy

DebtClear is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us at the address below.

7

Your Data Rights

Deleting locally stored debt data: You can remove debts, payment history, and app settings from your device in either of these ways:

  • Clear app data: Go to Settings → Apps → DebtClear / KrediErit → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all debts, payment history, and settings while keeping the app installed.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling removes the app and its local data. Android backups, if enabled, are managed separately through your device and Google account settings.

We cannot access or recover your locally stored debt records. Data already processed by Google services is separate from the app's local database and is subject to those services' retention and deletion policies.

Messaging and analytics data: Disabling payment reminders or Android notification permission does not automatically delete Firebase identifiers, erase analytics records, or turn off Analytics. If you have a privacy or deletion request concerning these services, contact us using the details below. Firebase explains its data handling and retention at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Ad personalization: Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.

For requests related to data processed by Google's services, please refer to myaccount.google.com.
